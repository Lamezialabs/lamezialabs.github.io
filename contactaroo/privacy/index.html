Contactaroo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

2. What this policy covers

Contactaroo adds product enquiry and quote-request forms to a merchant's online store. This policy covers information handled when a merchant installs and uses Contactaroo, and when a shopper submits one of its forms. The merchant configures the form and receives the enquiry; we process shopper information on the merchant's behalf to provide the app.

3. Information we handle

From shoppers who submit an enquiry

  • Name and email address.
  • The message, and answers to any extra form fields the merchant has set up.
  • Product context sent by the storefront form: product and variant identifiers and titles, page URL, image, SKU, and the displayed price and currency.
  • Whether it is a question or a quote request, and when it was sent.

Contactaroo does not store a shopper's IP address or browser details with the enquiry. It sends no confirmation email to the shopper; the shopper sees an on-screen confirmation.

To limit spam, the form includes a hidden field that real shoppers do not see or fill in. See section 4.

From merchants

  • Shop domain, and the Shopify session created at install, including the access token and the Shopify user details provided at sign-in.
  • The notification email address the merchant enters, and whether it has been verified.
  • App settings: form fields, wording and appearance.
  • Billing status and trial dates read from Shopify. We do not receive payment card details.
  • Whether the Contactaroo block has been activated in the merchant's theme.

Shopify access

Contactaroo requests one access scope, write_app_proxy. It does not read the merchant's customers, orders or product catalogue through the Shopify Admin API. Its limited use of the Admin API is to:

  • check the store's app subscription and, for a $0 subscription, whether the store is a Shopify partner development store; and
  • write a Contactaroo-owned metafield on the app installation holding the merchant's form and appearance settings, so the storefront form can display them. It contains no shopper enquiries.

4. How we use information

  • To show each enquiry in the merchant's Contactaroo dashboard and email it to the merchant.
  • To send a one-time email verifying the merchant's notification address.
  • To operate the app: settings, billing and installation checks, limits on the number of enquiries per customer email and per store per hour, and recovery from errors.
  • To handle privacy requests received from Shopify, merchants and shoppers.

We do not sell personal information and do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Spam protection

The storefront form includes a hidden field that real shoppers do not see or fill in. If it is filled in, the submission is treated as automated, is acknowledged as if it had been received, and is not saved or emailed to the merchant. Enquiries are also limited per shopper email address and per store per hour, and a genuine shopper who reaches a limit is asked to try again later. These checks do not collect or store IP addresses.

5. Notification emails

Each enquiry is emailed to the merchant's verified notification address, with the shopper's email address set as the reply-to address. Emails are sent from "Contactaroo" at notifications@lamezialabs.com and contain the enquiry details, including the shopper's name, email address and message.

Once delivered, an email is a copy held by the merchant and the merchant's email provider. It is outside Contactaroo's control and is not removed when an enquiry is deleted or expires in the app.

6. Service providers and international transfers

  • Google Cloud (Cloud Run, Cloud Tasks, Cloud Scheduler, Secret Manager): hosting and background jobs. Our production hosting is intended to be in a European region.
  • Neon (PostgreSQL): application database, hosted by Neon on Amazon Web Services. Our production database is intended to be in a European region.
  • Resend (operated by Plus Five Five, Inc., United States): sends enquiry and verification emails. Resend states that the data it stores is held in the United States, whichever sending region is used.
  • Shopify: platform, authentication and billing, under Shopify's own terms and privacy policy.

Our servers and database are intended to be in Europe, but some information is processed in the United States by Resend, and our providers may access their systems from other countries. Where personal information is transferred outside the United Kingdom or the European Economic Area, we rely on the safeguards our providers offer, such as the international data transfer terms in their data processing agreements (Resend states that its agreement includes the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and that it participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework).

These providers keep standard technical logs. Our application logs use opaque identifiers and fixed codes and are designed not to include shoppers' names, email addresses or messages; a store's domain name may appear in them. Google Cloud keeps application logs for 30 days by default. Resend keeps email and log data for a period that depends on its plan and settings. Our database provider also keeps technical logs under its own retention settings.

7. Retention and deletion

Enquiries in the app

  • Automatic expiry. An enquiry becomes due for permanent erasure from our database 90 days after it was submitted, and is erased by the retention process described below.
  • Merchant deletion. When a merchant deletes an enquiry it disappears from the Contactaroo dashboard immediately. It cannot be recovered or viewed by the merchant afterwards. The database record is kept, hidden, until our retention process permanently erases it. This is the earlier of 30 days after the merchant deleted it and 90 days after the enquiry was submitted.
  • Timing of erasure. The retention process is designed to run once a day. A record is erased the first time the process runs successfully after its deadline. We therefore do not guarantee erasure at an exact time: it can occur up to about a day after the deadline, and later if a run is delayed or fails. Until then a hidden record remains in our database and is covered by privacy requests described below.

Backups and delivered emails

  • Database backups. Our database provider keeps a change history so the database can be restored to an earlier point. Erased records may remain in that history until it expires. This period is limited and set by our database plan and configuration. We do not restore erased enquiries for merchants.
  • Emails already delivered are not affected, as explained in section 5.

Uninstalling the app

When a merchant uninstalls Contactaroo we immediately delete the Shopify session and billing state and reset the theme-activation state. The store's enquiries, settings and store record remain until Shopify sends its shop/redact request (about 48 hours after uninstall, according to Shopify), when we delete all remaining data for that store. If that request is not received, enquiries are still erased by the 90-day limit.

8. Shopify privacy requests

  • Customer erasure (customers/redact). We delete that store's enquiries, including hidden deleted ones, matching the customer's email address (case-insensitive), and related request records.
  • Store erasure (shop/redact). We delete all of that store's data held by Contactaroo.
  • Customer data requests (customers/data_request). The request is received and recorded, but no export is sent automatically. A designated person at Lamezia Labs reviews each request, verifies the store owner through a Shopify-confirmed channel, and provides matching data still held to the store owner, who responds to the shopper. We aim to act within Shopify's 30-day requirement. The record of the request, which can hold the customer's email address for matching, has that address cleared when the request is completed or expires, and finished records are then removed. Unresolved requests keep a limited administrative record, without the customer's email address, for a longer period.

9. Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. Shopify webhooks and storefront requests are signature-verified. Shopify session tokens are stored in the database and are not separately encrypted by the application. They rely on the database provider's encryption at rest; Neon documents that stored data is encrypted at rest. No system is completely secure.

10. Your rights and contact

Under UK data protection law you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, to object to or restrict its use, and to receive a copy of it. Shoppers should first contact the merchant whose store they wrote to, as the merchant receives the enquiry. You can also contact us at info@lamezialabs.com. If you are unhappy with how your information has been handled, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
