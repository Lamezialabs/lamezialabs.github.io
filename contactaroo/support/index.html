Contactaroo Help and Support

Contactaroo adds Ask a Question and Get a Quote forms to your Shopify product pages and sends each enquiry to your inbox.

Need help? Email info@lamezialabs.com. Include your .myshopify.com address, what you were trying to do and the steps that led to the problem. Please do not send passwords, access tokens or shopper details you do not need to share.

Get started

  1. Install Contactaroo from the Shopify App Store and follow the Shopify authorisation and plan approval screens. Contactaroo uses your Shopify login; there is no separate account.
  2. Open Settings and enter the email address that should receive enquiries. We send a verification email to that address; open its link to verify it. Notifications are sent only to a verified address.
  3. On the Contactaroo Home page, choose Quick Install. In the theme editor, review the block and save your theme.
  4. Open one of your product pages and submit a test enquiry to check the whole flow.

To add the button yourself, go to Online Store > Themes > Customize, open a product template, add the Contactaroo Ask a Question app block, and save. Contactaroo needs the Online Store sales channel.

Ask a Question and Get a Quote

Both forms use the same button and form. In the Quick Install settings on the Home page, choose Ask a Question for general product questions or Get a Quote for price requests. Quote requests are marked as quotes in your Enquiries list and in the notification email.

  • Each enquiry includes the shopper's name, email and message, plus the product, selected variant, page link, and the SKU, image and price shown on the page where available.
  • You can add up to eight extra form fields: short text, long text, number, dropdown or checkbox.
  • In the theme editor you can change the button text, colours, corner radius, alignment and width, choose a simple button or a larger enquiry section, and edit the section wording.
  • In Settings you can change the confirmation message shoppers see after sending.

Notification emails

Each enquiry is emailed to your verified notification address from notifications@lamezialabs.com. The shopper's email address is set as the reply-to address, so replying to the notification writes to the shopper. In Settings you can edit the email subject and introduction.

Manage your enquiries

  • Open Enquiries to see every enquiry, newest first. Search by customer name, email, product or message.
  • Open an enquiry to see the full message, the customer, the product and variant, and any extra answers. Use Reply by email or copy the customer's email address.
  • You can delete one or several enquiries. A deleted enquiry disappears from Contactaroo immediately and cannot be restored in the app. Enquiries are not kept indefinitely; see the privacy policy for retention details.
  • Emails already delivered to your inbox are not removed when you delete an enquiry in Contactaroo.

Troubleshooting

The button is not visible on a product page
Check that the Contactaroo block was added to the product template and that you saved the theme. Make sure the button is switched on in the block settings.
Quick Install does not confirm activation
Save the theme editor changes, then check the Home page again. The check can take a short time.
No notification email arrived
Check that your notification address is verified in Settings and look in your spam or junk folder. If the verification email did not arrive, request it again after a minute. Enquiries are always listed in the Enquiries page, even if an email is delayed.
A shopper could not send an enquiry
Messages must contain between 10 and 4,000 characters and a valid email address. Contactaroo also limits repeated or very frequent submissions to protect you from spam, so a shopper who sends many enquiries in an hour may be asked to try again later.
The form shows a subscription error
Contactaroo needs an active subscription. Check your plan in Shopify admin.

Billing and uninstalling

Shopify shows the plan and trial terms when you approve the plan. Manage or cancel your subscription through Shopify. To uninstall, remove Contactaroo in Shopify admin, and remove the app block from your theme if it is still there.
